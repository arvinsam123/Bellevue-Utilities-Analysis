# Bellevue Utilities Analysis

**Why did my electricity bill rise when I used less power?**

I analyzed two years of apartment utility costs in Bellevue, Washington.
Electricity use fell 11.4% across matched billing periods, while the PSE bill
rose 9.6%. I separated the effects of usage and price, estimated how much of
the usage decline was associated with weather, and compared spending with
published local benchmarks.
